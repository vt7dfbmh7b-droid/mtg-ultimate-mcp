# V0.15 Promotion Precommitment Contract

_Status: experimental / shadow-only. Frozen before any genuine future holdout is sealed._

This document explains the production review contract implemented by the V0.15 future-evidence pipeline. It is not stable-release authorization.

## Claim scope

The current promotion experiment is deliberately narrow:

> **Predict TopDeck event-top-cut outcome from strict promotion-grade prospective tournament evidence using the frozen structural projection below.**

It is **not** a universal Commander deck-strength model, a pilot-skill model, or proof that a card/deck is objectively best.

## Frozen promotion features

The current production projection contains exactly:

- `manaEfficiency`
- `interactionEfficiency`

The generic learning layer may know other feature names, but promotion-grade records are rejected if they contain missing or additional projection keys under this contract.

Any future feature expansion must create a new precommitment before observing the future holdout that will evaluate it.

## Production training corpus minimums

A system-clock production seal requires:

- at least **200** strict historical/prospective training records;
- minority outcome share at least **0.20**;
- at least **10** unique provider events;
- at least **20** unique provider pilots;
- no single leakage group above **0.25** of training records;
- explicit provider event identity on every training row;
- explicit provider pilot identity on every training row;
- the existing strict source/target, temporal provenance, historical legality, exact-deck, and replayability gates.

Injected-clock test seals may use smaller synthetic corpora, but they remain permanently test-attested and can never support a genuine future promotion claim.

## Future holdout minimums

Before evidence can reach human promotion review, the future holdout must contain:

- at least **200** genuinely post-seal records;
- minority outcome share at least **0.20**;
- at least **10** unique provider events;
- at least **20** unique provider pilots;
- no single leakage group above **0.25**;
- complete provider event/pilot identity;
- no overlap with training leakage groups, provider events, pilots, or exact deck fingerprints;
- the exact sealed feature extractor and training-fitted normalizer.

## Model-quality gates

The neural shadow model must satisfy all of the following:

### Absolute floors

- balanced accuracy at least **0.60**;
- AUROC at least **0.65**.

### Versus transparent model

- balanced-accuracy gain at least **0.02**;
- AUROC gain at least **0.01**;
- no log-loss regression;
- no Brier-score regression.

### Versus prevalence baseline

- balanced-accuracy gain at least **0.05**;
- no log-loss regression.

### Calibration

- expected calibration error no greater than **0.15**.

All required metrics must be finite. A relative neural gain is not enough if the absolute model remains weak.

## Exact code/dependency identity

A genuine production seal also freezes:

- repository full name;
- exact 40-character Git commit SHA;
- SHA-256 of `package-lock.json`;
- `.node-version`;
- evaluator-contract version.

Future holdout construction and future evaluation first pull the immutable seal, extract the frozen Git SHA, then checkout that exact revision before `npm ci`, build, materialization, or evaluation. The runtime identity is recomputed and must match the seal.

This protects against accidental post-seal model/policy/dependency drift. Repository governance is still required: no application-level mechanism can defend against an authorized actor deliberately rewriting the workflow to bypass its own safeguards.

## Baseline versus legacy usefulness signal

The older sealed evaluator's `usefulness` field is retained for diagnostics/backward compatibility. It can report a relative shadow gain.

The production promotion-aware path applies this stronger contract **above** that signal. A legacy `shadow-gain-observed` result is therefore insufficient by itself to reach human review.

## Release boundary

Even when every model/evidence gate passes:

- `promotionAuthorized` remains false;
- automatic stable promotion remains false;
- `src/server-current.ts` is not changed automatically;
- stable version is not bumped automatically;
- merge to `main` is not authorized automatically;
- explicit user-approved release review is still required.

Hard MTG truth always remains outside and above learned output.
