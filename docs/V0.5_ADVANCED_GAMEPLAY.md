# MTG Ultimate V0.5 — Advanced Gameplay Intelligence

V0.5 builds on the hard Commander legality and rules-aware V0.4 consistency engine by adding a more detailed **card/payment/interaction/combat state layer**.

The design rule remains the same: **known Magic rules are kept separate from simulation assumptions and heuristics**.

## New V0.5 MCP tools

### `card_intelligence_v05`

A consolidated card report combining:

- current Oracle/card identity
- Commander legality and color identity
- strategic role classification
- practical best-use guidance
- synergy hooks
- advanced casting/payment mechanics
- interaction/protection profile
- combat characteristics for creatures
- commander-dependent text
- rules-attention notes
- optional fit against one or two supplied commanders

This is intended to answer normal questions such as:

- What does this card actually do in my deck?
- What is its best use?
- What does it synergize with?
- Can it go in this Commander deck?
- Is there a rules trap I should know about?

### `analyze_card_casting_v05`

Detects common casting/payment mechanics including:

- convoke
- improvise
- delve
- affinity
- Phyrexian mana
- named alternative costs such as evoke/escape/blitz/overload/prototype/sneak when detectable from Oracle text
- pitch-style “rather than pay this spell’s mana cost” text
- “without paying its mana cost” permissions
- immediate and recurring Treasure creation

The report keeps mana value separate from the actual amount/resources used to cast a spell.

### `analyze_deck_casting_v05`

Inventories those payment/resource mechanics across a resolved deck and preserves card-level evidence.

Treasure output is reported as text-derived resource evidence rather than assumed guaranteed mana in every game.

### `evaluate_castability_v05`

A state-aware payment solver.

Inputs can include:

- white/blue/black/red/green/colorless mana
- flexible “any color” mana
- Treasures
- untapped creatures and their colors for convoke
- untapped artifacts for improvise
- graveyard card count for delve
- affinity count
- life for Phyrexian mana
- whether the spell is the commander
- current commander tax
- whether the non-mana requirement for an alternative/free line is available

The solver evaluates normal and supported alternative/free lines separately.

**Commander tax remains an applicable additional cost even when an alternative cost or “without paying its mana cost” permission is used.**

### `evaluate_interaction_exchange_v05`

Evaluates a named threat, a named answer, and an optional named protection response.

Current high-confidence patterns include:

- hard counters
- “unless they pay” soft counters
- noncreature/creature/instant-sorcery counter restrictions
- destroy
- exile
- bounce
- damage-based targeted interaction
- board-wipe detection
- hexproof
- indestructible
- phasing
- uncounterable effects
- countering the interaction spell itself

Results are labelled **definite**, **conditional**, or **unlikely**.

### `simulate_combat_snapshot_v05`

A single-combat board snapshot using named attackers/blockers.

Current supported combat information includes:

- printed numeric power/toughness
- flying
- reach
- menace
- trample
- double strike
- first strike awareness
- deathtouch
- lifelink metadata
- vigilance metadata
- unblockable text
- commander-damage tracking for named commander attackers

Variable/non-numeric stats such as `*` are surfaced as unresolved instead of being treated as zero.

### `analyze_commander_dependencies_v05`

Finds cards whose Oracle text directly references or depends on the commander.

Examples include:

- “if you control your commander”
- “as long as you control your commander”
- effects that modify a commander you control
- commander-damage references

This provides a clearer way to evaluate whether repeated commander removal hurts only access to the commander or also shuts down supporting cards.

## Casting/payment model rules

V0.5 follows the current Magic cost model in broad structure:

1. Choose the normal or applicable alternative cost.
2. Add applicable additional costs such as commander tax.
3. Apply supported reductions such as affinity.
4. Pay the resulting cost with the available payment mechanisms.

Supported payment behavior includes:

- convoke paying generic or a creature’s color
- improvise paying generic only
- delve paying generic by exiling graveyard cards
- affinity reducing generic cost based on a supplied relevant count
- Phyrexian mana paid with matching mana or 2 life per supported symbol
- Treasure as expendable flexible mana

## Interaction model philosophy

The V0.5 interaction engine does not declare an arbitrary three-card stack fully solved unless its recognized rules pattern supports that conclusion.

For example:

- indestructible is a definite answer to a simple destroy effect
- indestructible is **not** protection from exile
- hexproof can stop opposing targeted removal but does not stop most untargeted board wipes
- a soft counter is conditional because the opposing player may be able to pay
- a counter restricted to noncreature spells does not counter a creature spell

Ward, replacement effects, modes, controller-dependent targeting, layers, split-second corner cases, triggered abilities, and unusual stack branches still require deeper state/rule modeling.

## Combat model philosophy

V0.5 deliberately starts with a transparent snapshot model rather than claiming to simulate an entire multiplayer combat phase perfectly.

The blocker assignment is greedy rather than game-theoretically optimal.

Exact combat can still depend on:

- counters
- equipment and auras
- anthem/lord effects
- continuous effects
- activated abilities
- pump/protection spells
- attack/block taxes and restrictions
- goad
- damage prevention/replacement
- first/double-strike ordering
- token creation during combat
- attack and damage triggers
- extra combat steps
- multiple defending players/planeswalkers/battles

Those cases are surfaced through caveats rather than hidden behind a fake precise answer.

## What remains for deeper V0.5/V0.6 simulation

The next major simulation work should connect these specialized engines into the per-turn Monte Carlo state machine:

1. Treasure generation and spending inside simulated turns
2. alternative/free-cost choices inside turn sequencing
3. convoke/improvise/delve/affinity inside the deck-level simulator
4. commander-dependent permanents reacting to commander removal/recast state
5. battlefield threat selection for opponent interaction
6. protection/counter exchanges on simulated key spells
7. richer combat board states, counters, equipment, lords, and attack triggers
8. graveyard/exile/battlefield requirements for combo assembly
9. ward and target-cost handling
10. deeper priority/stack branches

V0.5 therefore materially improves card-level and supplied-state gameplay reasoning while remaining explicit about the boundary between a high-confidence rules model and a complete digital implementation of Magic.
