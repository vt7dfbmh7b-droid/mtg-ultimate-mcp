# MTG Ultimate V0.4 — Commander Rules + Advanced Simulation

V0.4 separates **hard Magic/Commander rules** from **simulation assumptions**.

## Hard Commander deck-construction rules

The `check_commander_rules` tool validates resolved decks before advanced simulation.

### Color identity

Every card in the deck must have a color identity that is a subset of the combined color identity of the commander(s).

Examples:

- A black-red (`BR`) commander may use black, red, black-red, and colorless cards.
- It may not use a white, blue, or green identity card.
- A hybrid `{B/R}` card has both black and red in its color identity under the current Commander rule.
- With two legal commanders, their color identities are combined.
- Lands with basic land types are checked so those intrinsic mana colors do not fall outside commander identity.

The engine uses Scryfall's Oracle-level `color_identity` field for card identity, which correctly separates Oracle/rules identity from physical printing identity.

### Commander eligibility and two-commander configurations

V0.4 recognizes:

- one ordinary legendary-creature commander
- cards whose Oracle text explicitly says they can be your commander
- Partner
- Friends forever
- Choose a Background + legendary Background
- Doctor's companion + Doctor
- matching Partner with pairs

Invalid two-commander pairings are rejected rather than silently combining their colors.

### Deck size, legality, and singleton

The engine checks:

- exactly 100 cards including commander(s)
- 99 library cards with one commander or 98 with two
- each designated commander appears once
- every resolved card is Commander-legal
- Commander banned/not-legal status
- singleton by English card name
- unlimited basic lands
- Oracle copy-count exceptions such as “a deck can have any number…”
- bounded copy-count exceptions such as “a deck can have up to nine…”

If cards cannot be resolved, the result is `incomplete`; the engine does not falsely declare the deck legal.

## New V0.4 MCP tools

### `check_commander_rules`

Full hard rules validation for a pasted Commander deck.

### `check_card_for_commander`

Answers questions such as:

- Can this blue card go in my Rakdos deck?
- Is this hybrid card legal with my commander?
- Is this land legal in this color identity?
- Are these two commanders a legal pair?

### `analyze_mana_base_v04`

Uses resolved Oracle text and land types to analyze:

- produced colors
- commander-identity mana sources
- shock lands
- check lands
- fast lands
- slow lands
- reveal lands
- multiplayer/bond lands
- unconditional tapped lands
- other conditional tapped lands without guessing
- restricted mana text
- detected cost reducers
- fetch/search lands
- exact fetch targets that actually exist in the submitted deck

A fetch target must satisfy the source card's search clause and be present as a resolved land in the deck. For example, a “Swamp or Mountain card” fetch does not gain imaginary access to an Island merely because the deck is being simulated.

### `simulate_pod_pressure_v04`

This tool first requires a fully resolved, Commander-legal deck. It then combines:

- V0.3 colored-mana consistency simulation
- commander first-cast affordability
- +2 and +4 commander-tax affordability
- configurable commander-removal pressure
- configurable key-spell interaction pressure
- configurable board-reset pressure
- commander recast proxies
- protection-density proxy for challenged combo attempts
- natural and tutor-assisted combo assembly from the underlying model

## Pod pressure profiles

Built-in profiles are:

- `goldfish`
- `casual`
- `core`
- `upgraded`
- `optimized`
- `cedh`

The numerical interaction rates are deliberately returned in every result. They are **scenario assumptions**, not universal observed frequencies and not claimed win rates.

Users can override:

- `commanderRemovalPerTurn`
- `keySpellInteractionChance`
- `boardResetPerTurn`

## Rule fact vs simulation assumption

MTG Ultimate keeps these separate:

**Rules facts**

- color identity
- deck size
- singleton restrictions
- card/commander format legality
- commander eligibility
- valid two-commander pairing mechanics
- actual Oracle text
- actual set / collector printing identity

**Simulation assumptions**

- how frequently opponents remove a commander
- how frequently a key spell is challenged
- how often a board reset occurs
- whether a protection card is likely to have been seen

This prevents an estimated gameplay behavior from being presented as if it were a Magic rule.

## Current V0.4 limitations

The advanced pressure layer still does not run a complete four-player rules engine. It does not yet fully choose exact opponent spells, model every priority pass, resolve all stack branches, understand table politics, or feed commander uptime into every commander-dependent permanent.

The next simulation work should focus on:

1. exact tutor target selection and timing
2. draw-engine activation and extra-card flow
3. alternate casting costs and cost reducers during turn sequencing
4. commander-only / creature-only / artifact-only mana restrictions during payment
5. exact common fetch/shock/check/fast/slow-land sequencing inside Monte Carlo iterations
6. board-state-aware removal targets
7. stack interaction with specific protection/counterspell exchanges
8. calibration against observed tournament/reference samples without converting correlation into fake causation
