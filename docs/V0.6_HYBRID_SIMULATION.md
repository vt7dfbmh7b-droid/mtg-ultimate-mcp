# V0.6 Hybrid Simulation

V0.6 adds a second, advanced turn-simulation lane without discarding the stronger V0.4 land/fetch/tutor consistency engine.

## Why hybrid

No lightweight simulator can accurately model every Magic rule, every opponent choice, and every card interaction at once. V0.6 therefore keeps two views:

- **V0.4 baseline** for opening hands, lands, colored mana, common conditional lands, fetch targets, tutors, draw flow, and commander affordability.
- **V0.6 advanced lane** for Treasures, special payment mechanics, commander pressure, key-spell interaction, and protection responses.

The public `simulate_advanced_gameplay_v06` tool combines both views.

## Advanced turn resources

The V0.6 lane carries these resources through turns:

- ordinary colored/colorless/flexible mana
- Treasure tokens
- graveyard-card count for delve / supported escape checks
- life for Phyrexian mana
- untapped non-mana creatures for convoke
- untapped non-mana artifacts for improvise
- artifacts/Treasures for supported artifact-affinity counts

Mana creatures are intentionally excluded from the same turn's convoke pool when being treated as mana sources, and mana rocks are excluded from improvise in the same abstraction. This avoids obvious double-spending.

## Casting sequence

For supported casts the advanced lane follows this broad sequence:

1. determine an affordable normal or supported alternative line
2. include commander tax when relevant
3. use the V0.5 payment solver for supported reductions/payment methods
4. actually spend mana/Treasures/life/graveyard resources
5. only after the spell has been paid for, model an opponent challenge
6. if challenged, protection must be affordable from the resources that remain
7. resolve or stop the spell

That ordering matters: mana spent on the original spell cannot also be used to protect it.

## Supported special payment/casting mechanics

Currently integrated into turn sequencing when sufficient information is available:

- Treasure
- convoke
- improvise
- delve
- artifact affinity
- Phyrexian mana
- evoke
- blitz
- overload
- prototype
- sneak
- escape when a parseable exile-card requirement is present

Generic pitch-style alternatives and “without paying its mana cost” permissions are intentionally not assumed to be active just because those words appear on the card. Their separate permission/resource state must be known before they can be treated as live.

## Commander pressure

The advanced lane tracks:

- commander casts
- accumulated commander tax
- commander spells stopped on the stack
- commander battlefield uptime
- modeled commander removal
- commander-dependent permanents deployed while playing the deck

This makes commander-centric decks easier to compare with decks that function more independently.

## Protection / interaction pressure

The pressure model may challenge key combo-piece spells and commanders. When that happens, the simulator looks for an affordable protection/counter card from the hand after paying for the original spell.

It also has a simplified commander-removal and board-wipe pressure pass.

The built-in profiles (`goldfish`, `casual`, `upgraded`, `optimized`, `cedh`) are assumptions for scenario testing. They are not claimed measured win rates or universal descriptions of those tables.

## Combo readiness

For user-supplied named combo pieces, V0.6 reports both:

- all named pieces having been seen by a given turn
- all named pieces currently being in hand/battlefield by a given turn

This is deliberately called **zone readiness**, not “the combo definitely wins.” Full Commander Spellbook prerequisites, targets, mana, timing, graveyard/exile state, and opponent interaction can require more information.

## Simple output

The MCP tool defaults to `detail: simple` and returns a short summary plus a few key numbers. `standard` and `detailed` modes expose more of the baseline/advanced results.

The intended user-facing explanation is practical:

- what helped the deck
- what caused it to stumble
- whether its commander survives/comes back reliably
- whether special mana/casting mechanics materially matter
- whether protection is actually available after committing to key plays

## V0.6 limits

Still outside the claimed model:

- full Magic layer system
- arbitrary replacement/prevention effects
- complete ward/controller targeting logic in Monte Carlo turns
- exhaustive X/mode/additional-cost handling
- full priority trees with several opponents responding in sequence
- exact political/strategic multiplayer choices
- all continuous combat modifications from counters, Auras, Equipment, lords, static effects, and combat tricks
- every zone/timing prerequisite for every combo

These are future deep-state improvements, not silently assumed capabilities.
