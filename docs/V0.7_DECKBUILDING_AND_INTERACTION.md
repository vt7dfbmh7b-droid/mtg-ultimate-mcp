# V0.7 — Deck construction and game-state intelligence

V0.7 has two linked goals:

1. make supplied game-state reasoning more useful without pretending to be a complete digital Magic implementation; and
2. make **building and upgrading Commander decks first-class workflows**, with legality, exact printings, budget/set/theme constraints, real-world references, and simulation evidence connected to the same system.

## Design principle

Rules facts, simulation assumptions, and observational evidence remain separate.

- **Rules facts** come from Oracle/card legality and the local Commander construction validator.
- **Game-state evaluators** solve supported state that was explicitly supplied and return conditional/unresolved when the state is insufficient.
- **Simulations** are models and expose their assumptions.
- **Community/tournament evidence** is observational. Repeated card/package/structure patterns can support a recommendation but do not prove causation.

Normal user-facing explanations should stay short. The internals can be complicated without forcing every answer to be complicated.

## Ward-aware target ranking

`rank_interaction_targets_v07` takes:

- one answer/removal card
- candidate permanents
- optional commander/combo/development flags
- optional resources available to pay Ward

The model first checks the detected target scope of the answer. It then scores legal targets using threat signals such as commander status, known combo participation, tutor/draw/control/mana/win-condition roles, and board development.

Supported Ward parsers include common:

- generic mana costs
- colored mana symbols
- life payments
- discard costs
- sacrifice-permanent costs

Ward is modeled as a trigger caused by the opponent targeting the permanent. It is not treated as a hexproof-style ban on choosing the target. Unsupported variable/card-specific Ward costs remain conditional.

## Multiplayer stack chains

`evaluate_multiplayer_stack_v07` accepts actions in cast order and evaluates common response chains in LIFO order.

V0.7 recognizes:

- common hard counters
- common soft/tax counters
- uncounterable text
- a simple chain of counters/protection from multiple players

It intentionally does not claim full coverage for split second, copies, retargeting, modes, every timing permission, or arbitrary priority branches.

## Combat modifiers

`analyze_combat_board_v07` extends the earlier combat snapshot with supplied board modifiers.

Supported V0.7 modifiers include:

- numeric printed power/toughness
- +1/+1 counters
- -1/-1 counters
- common static Equipment `equipped creature gets +N/+N` effects
- common static Aura `enchanted creature gets +N/+N` effects
- common lord effects such as `other Vampire creatures you control get +1/+1`
- common granted combat keywords found in those static effects

Variable printed stats such as `*` stay unresolved rather than being silently converted to zero. Full layer ordering, characteristic-defining abilities, temporary tricks, attachment legality/costs, and arbitrary continuous effects remain later work.

## Zone-aware combo readiness

`evaluate_combo_zones_v07` distinguishes “the card has been seen” from “the combo piece is actually usable now.”

Supported zones:

- hand
- battlefield
- graveyard
- exile
- command zone
- library
- stack

The evaluator can use explicit required zones and detects common graveyard/exile permissions/keywords. A card in the library is not assembled without a search line, and a card in exile is not automatically castable merely because it is exiled.

Zone readiness is only one prerequisite. Mana, targets, priority, summoning sickness, timing, activation costs, once-per-turn limits, and opponent interaction may still stop the line.

## Building a Commander deck from scratch

`build_commander_deck_v07` promotes full deck construction to a first-class MCP workflow.

Inputs can include:

- one or two resolved commanders, optionally by exact set + collector number
- target bracket
- Scryfall theme query
- maximum USD price per selected card
- allowed set codes
- excluded cards
- must-include cards
- land count
- maximum nonbasic-land count
- optional Archidekt reference decks
- optional post-build simulation

The builder:

1. derives the combined commander color identity;
2. searches only Commander-legal/color-identity-legal candidates;
3. gathers role pools for ramp, draw/selection, interaction, protection, tutors, recursion, wipes, and early action;
4. uses bracket-targeted role-density heuristics plus Scryfall/EDHREC signals to rank candidates;
5. fills nonland slots while respecting singleton names and explicit exclusions/includes;
6. builds a legal mana-base draft from nonbasics plus appropriate basics;
7. emits the exact selected `(SET) collector` printing on every selected line;
8. runs the hard Commander validator over the result;
9. optionally requests Commander Spellbook bracket evidence;
10. optionally simulates the completed draft and compares it with attributed Archidekt references.

The first generated list is called a **draft** deliberately. It is not claimed to be globally optimal. The next optimization stage can use repeated build → simulate → replace passes and stronger commander-specific synergy discovery.

### Printing and price behavior

Rules use Oracle identity, while shopping/value uses physical printing identity.

The builder always reports the exact printing it selected. When `maxUsdPerCard` is supplied, Scryfall price filtering is used during candidate searches. Without an explicit price objective, the selected printing is not claimed to be the globally cheapest printing in Magic history.

The older upgrade engine remains stronger at explicitly searching multiple releases for a budget-qualifying printing of an upgrade candidate.

## Simulation-backed upgrades

`plan_commander_upgrade_v07` connects the existing printing-aware upgrade candidate engine with whole-deck replacement and simulation.

The workflow:

1. resolves and hard-validates the current Commander deck;
2. identifies structural deficits;
3. searches legal candidate additions under bracket, set, theme, exclusion, and price constraints;
4. protects cards the player says must not be cut;
5. chooses a bounded number of candidate IN/OUT swaps;
6. rebuilds the complete candidate decklist with exact printings;
7. resolves and re-validates the candidate list;
8. runs the original and candidate lists with the **same seed** and simulation settings;
9. reports comparable signals such as functional keeps, commander uptime, protection success, spells cast, and Treasure use;
10. optionally adds bracket and community-reference evidence.

A positive simulation delta is support for a recommendation, not an automatic command to make the swap. Theme, preferred win routes, fun cards, budget, and meta needs can outweigh a small numeric gain.

## Tournament-informed pressure calibration

`simulate_calibrated_gameplay_v07` can optionally query the existing TopDeck.gg tournament-analysis layer, inspect the structural metrics of the higher-performing sampled cohort, and map those metrics to the closest existing V0.6 pressure preset.

The transparent structural score currently uses:

- cheap/free interaction density
- total interaction density
- protection density
- tutor density
- fast-mana density

This is **not** a measured counterspell/removal probability from actual game logs. A tournament decklist tells us what was registered, not what was drawn, held up, cast, or targeted in a particular game. The calibration therefore reports low/medium confidence and its caveats.

When tournament access/data is unavailable, the tool falls back to the requested named pressure preset rather than failing the whole simulation.

## Important V0.7 limits

Still intentionally outside the claimed model:

- a full replacement/prevention/layer engine
- arbitrary X/modes/additional costs and copy effects
- every retarget/priority/split-second branch
- full combat layer ordering and all continuous effects
- multiplayer politics and fully strategic attack/defender selection
- exact timing/activation prerequisites for every combo in Magic
- perfect causal inference from tournament outcomes
- automatic commander-specific theme understanding for every commander without supplied theme/reference context
- globally optimal cheapest-printing selection for every from-scratch card unless price constraints specifically drive the search

## Next construction priorities

A strong V0.8 direction is to make deck creation iterative rather than one-pass:

1. derive commander-specific synergy hooks from Oracle text;
2. collect community/tournament reference packages where appropriate;
3. construct multiple candidate lists;
4. run same-seed simulation and bracket/legality checks;
5. replace weak cards/packages;
6. repeat until improvements flatten or constraints stop further changes;
7. produce a clean final list plus a short explanation of its game plan and main win routes.

This keeps deck building, upgrading, and simulation as one connected feedback loop instead of three unrelated tools.
