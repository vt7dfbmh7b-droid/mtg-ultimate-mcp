## Overall verdict

**REJECT as a new accepted Commander product baseline.**

Keep `e17b0a1cba659b229fd6f0b6e2df79c5e464a616` as the latest accepted fully validated Commander product baseline.

The `005717...` repair is still useful engineering work and should be retained as evidence: it closes the specific context-dead low-curve utility failure that previously allowed an unusable color-restricted cost reducer to survive in a Gruul Bello deck through generic low-curve/structural lanes. In the current Animated Army output, that decisive Jet Medallion failure is gone.

The result is target movement and sometimes bracket movement without consistently preserving or improving the deck's actual strategic identity.

## Cross-fixture diagnosis

The repeated generic weakness is now better described as:

**mechanism-aware relative replacement value remains too weak, especially on the OUT/cut side.**

The system can identify many useful incoming role cards and satisfy hard component floors, but it does not consistently recognize that an outgoing card may be strategically important because it directly embodies the requested deck mechanism, typal density, Aura package, or commander-specific permanent shape.

Repeated evidence:
- Ellivere: defining Aura/enchantment pieces are treated as expendable while broad value/token/interaction cards enter.
- Hakbal: Merfolk/typal engines and draw creatures are displaced by generic interaction, blink, tutors and a Sword.
- Lathril: Elf payoff/typal cards are displaced by aristocrats utility, non-Elf interaction and Equipment.
- Bello: qualifying high-MV artifact/enchantment mechanism pieces are displaced by low-cost generic structural cards.

A key diagnostic signal is that `cutStrategyAffinityScore` is often zero or very low for cards whose deck-specific importance is obvious from the request/commander mechanism. This suggests the next generic repair should not simply add more incoming-card affinity or raise hard theme minimums. It should improve **relative IN-vs-OUT mechanism/component importance**, including direct requested-component/commander-mechanism value of the card being cut.

## Next justified product action

A generic repair is justified because the same failure appears across several contrasting families.

The next smallest capability should:

1. identify direct requested-component and commander-mechanism affinity for **outgoing as well as incoming** cards;
2. compare IN versus OUT on that mechanism/component value when both can satisfy the structural need;
3. apply an advisory cut penalty / replacement preference for identity-bearing cards above the existing hard preservation floors;
4. preserve the ability to make necessary structural cuts when no credible strategy-compatible alternative exists;
5. avoid card-name, fixture-name and commander-specific exceptions;
6. add generic regressions spanning Aura/enchantment, typal, counters/explore-style, token/combat and high-MV artifact/enchantment mechanism cases;
7. pass focused + full immutable validation before another frozen-source replay.

Do **not** simply raise global theme minimums, freeze all typal/theme cards, add more hard vetoes, or increase candidate breadth as the repair.

After the repeated replacement-value defect is addressed and replayed, broaden BENCH-01 beyond these five precons to fresh combo, hybrid, control, aristocrats, budget and unusual commander incentives, with strong general-AI comparison where practical.
