## Overall verdict

**REJECT as a new accepted Commander product baseline.**

Keep `e17b0a1cba659b229fd6f0b6e2df79c5e464a616` as the latest accepted fully validated Commander baseline.

The `4a7bbf6...` code change is technically correct and remains useful generic regression coverage: when an outgoing card already has an exact modeled strategy-component role, losing that role now activates strategy preservation even below maximum numeric cut protection. But the five-deck replay shows that this does not address the dominant real-world failure. Most identity-bearing cards in the failing precons do not arrive at the pairing layer with an exact requested/commander-mechanism role at all. Their `cutRoles` are frequently empty or merely generic (`creature`, `equipment`, etc.), so `unreplacedStrategyComponentRoles` stays empty and the new categorical preservation condition never fires.

This is not a reason to revert the repair. It is evidence that the next generic defect sits one layer earlier: **requested-component / commander-mechanism recognition for outgoing cards is incomplete, especially for typal identity, Aura/enchantment identity and commander-specific permanent-shape incentives.**

## Cross-fixture diagnosis

The replay makes the architecture boundary much clearer.

`4a7bbf6...` repaired **preservation of already-recognized exact components**. The repeated failures occur because important outgoing cards are often **not recognized as requested/commander components in the first place**.

Repeated evidence across contrasting families:
- Ellivere: Aura/enchantment cards can arrive with no Aura/enchantment component role or only incidental generic roles.
- Hakbal: Merfolk/explore/counter engines can arrive as generic creatures or with no direct requested-component role.
- Lathril: an Elf combat payoff such as Timberwatch Elf can arrive as only `creature`, with zero strategy affinity.
- Bello: qualifying high-MV artifact/enchantment mechanisms can arrive with no commander-shape role at all.

Therefore the remaining repeated generic weakness is now:

**outgoing requested-component / commander-mechanism recognition is too shallow before relative replacement scoring.**

This is a role-inference / commander-specific synergy-recognition problem feeding replacement priority, not evidence that the downstream preservation veto should simply be made harder.

## Next justified product action

A further generic repair is justified because the same missing-signal pattern appears across Aura/enchantment, typal/counters, typal/tokens and artifact/enchantment commander-mechanism fixtures.

The next smallest capability should:

1. derive requested-component membership for outgoing cards using the same substantive request/theme evidence available to incoming candidates, rather than relying only on coarse `card.roles`;
2. recognize typal membership/payoff identity, Aura/enchantment identity and other explicit compound-request components symmetrically for IN and OUT;
3. expose commander-specific mechanism/card-shape affinity where the existing strategy model has substantive evidence (for example a commander caring about a permanent type/mana-value shape), without commander-name hardcoding;
4. feed that evidence into the existing advisory relative replacement/preservation layer rather than creating blanket hard vetoes;
5. keep structural fallback available when no credible on-plan cut/replacement exists;
6. add focused generic tests proving outgoing requested-component recognition across Aura/enchantment, typal, counters/explore-style and artifact/enchantment shape cases, plus controls showing necessary structural cuts still occur;
7. run the complete immutable validation suite;
8. only after green validation, freeze the exact product SHA and use a new representative replay/manual whole-deck review before accepting a baseline.

Do not simply raise theme floors, freeze all typal/enchantment cards, add card/commander-specific exceptions, or repeat this identical five-precon replay after a superficial wording-only change.
